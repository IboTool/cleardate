## 6. Structure and model concerns (not changed)

- **Range-only evidence:** the schema permits nullable numbers, but the current validator rejects min/max if typical is null. Sublocade 43-60 days, Brixadi weekly3-5/monthly19-26 days, and Vivitrol5-10 days cannot be entered as usable range-only values without inventing a typical. Their specific reported label ranges remain in sourceNotes.input, releaseHalfLifeHours null. This is a representational gap, not failure to find the product labels.

- **Release versus apparent elimination:** ER and post-patch terminal half-life are not independently identified release constants. Fentanyl retains the migration 23.5 h skin-release proxy as estimate; Butrans no separate release constant. Butrans is corrected to168 h wear. Other patch/depot/ER products outside the completed categories were not researched.

- **Suboxone/Zubsolv generic combination identity:** one unchanged component list spans different film/tablet products and strengths. EMA describes ~3% naloxone SL tablet bioavailability but 7-9% solution, with buprenorphine solution40-42%; DailyMed documents film/tablet differences. Both component bioavailabilities remain null rather than generalizing across dosage forms. Null does not mean complete absorption.

- **Phenotype formation maps:** CPIC text gives PM morphine AUC96% lower against combined NM/IM comparator, and postoperative (+)-M1 median AUC0 vs67 in PM/NM. These are not complete NM-normalized PM/IM/NM/UM systemic formation maps. Maps remain null. Genuine zero metabolite formation cannot currently be expressed by the validator positive-only phenotype map; no small epsilon was invented. The opened document identifies itself as a CPIC preprint; check the final published guideline before clinical use.

- **Legacy section-level migration:** old CYP2D6 sections tagged checked sometimes described a checked substrate pathway but explicitly left inhibitor status unassessed in text. Migration kept `inhibitor: none` on the strength of the section tag. Those carried opioid safety fields were outside the new-field-only Part A scope and should be reviewed individually; they must not be interpreted as newly established negative findings.

- **Clorazepate:** presystemic conversion is not represented by a compound intrinsic half-life. Parent remains unknown rather than assigning nordiazepam half-life to it. Component/link identities preserved.

- **Intrathecal baclofen:** generic route combines screening bolus and chronic infusion; unit corrected mg -> micrograms for bolus. Label CSF mean1.51 h is not systemic oral5.7 h. Current one-compound systemic kinetics and generic bolus product do not represent a pump or CSF clearance; no product split/code change made.

- **Combination ingredients still absent:** butalbital combination candidate tracks only butalbital, not acetaminophen/caffeine; pentazocine and several opioid brands also need ingredient review. Components were not added.

- **Metabolite identities/links:** hydroxyetizolam lacks hydroxyl position; zopiclone racemate and eszopiclone S metabolites share records; alprazolam hydroxy names may not uniquely specify isomer. Diazepam label mentions minor direct oxazepam formation, but original link structure was preserved. Temazepam minor N-desmethyl conjugate cannot be assigned wholesale to unconjugated oxazepam from recovery percentages alone.

- **Existing prior-pass concerns remain:** methadone/EMDP route, missing norbuprenorphine glucuronide and further dextromethorphan products, and kratom mixture versus isolated alkaloid identities were not repaired. Secondary categories and candidate IDs remain unchanged.
